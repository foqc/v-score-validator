# Example: thin evidence should lower willingness-to-pay

Keywords: willingness-to-pay, evidence, market, conservative-rating

When the idea description asserts customers will pay but gives no price signal,
existing spend, or comparable products, rate `pay` conservatively (typically ≤ 4)
and call out the missing evidence in the market audit. Do not invent market research.
This learning is supplemental and must never change the official recommendation matrix.
